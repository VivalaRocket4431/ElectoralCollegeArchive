What this archive contains — and what it doesn't

Fully sourced right now: for all seven elections from 2000 through 2024, the winning ticket and electoral vote count in every state and D.C.; the national popular vote; and the difference between electors pledged and electors' votes actually cast, including every faithless elector and abstention.

Elector names: complete, name-by-name rosters are loaded for nine states in 2024 — Arizona, Georgia, Michigan, Minnesota, Montana, Nevada, North Carolina, Pennsylvania and Wisconsin — each read directly off that state's Certificate of Ascertainment and checked against its electoral vote count. The remaining states are not missing from history: their certificates are public, permanent, and linked from every state panel here. They simply haven't been read into this site yet.

Not here yet: elections before 2000; state-by-state popular vote counts; voter registration by party. Nothing on this site is estimated or filled in by guesswork — if a number isn't sourced, it isn't shown.

  • Electoral votes and state outcomes: certified state results, cross-checked against the National Archives' Electoral College records for each year.
  • Elector rosters: NARA Certificates of Vote and Certificates of Ascertainment.
  • National popular vote: commonly published certified totals; these should be re-verified against official state canvasses before citation in published work.
